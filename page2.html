Social Media and food advice

Helpful Food Advice

Now even when social media gives not so great advice, some of it can actually be accurate or helpful.
For example, some people give advice on how to eat healthy on a budget, or how to meal prep for the week.
Some people even give advice on how to cook certain foods or recipes that are healthy and delicious.
There are also some professional nutritionists who give advice on social media that is backed by science and research.

While I can't grant the recommended influencers for food advice since there is a lot of them, here are some food advice that is accurate:

  • Eat more fibre
  • Stay hydrated
  • Eat in moderation
  • Try something new
  • Eat what makes you feel good
  • Listen to your body
  • Seek out reliable sources of information

You and I are both human beings when need food to survive.
Food helps us grow, stay healthy, gives us energy, and makes us feel good.
Food isn't our enemy and shouldn't be treated as such.
